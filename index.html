Hermes Agent

Hermes Agent is a private personal assistant operated by its owner for personal productivity automation.

Privacy Policy Terms of Service

What Hermes Agent Does

Hermes Agent helps its owner manage personal productivity workflows such as email triage, calendar briefings, deadline tracking, document drafting, spreadsheet updates, and file lookup. It is not a public SaaS product and is not offered for general public use.

Google Workspace Access

With the user's authorization, Hermes Agent connects to Google Workspace APIs to help manage Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, and Google Contacts. Google user data is accessed only to complete user-directed tasks or configured private automations.

Data Use

Google user data is not sold, shared for advertising, or used to train public AI models. Access can be revoked at any time from the user's Google Account permissions page.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, contact the app owner through the developer contact email listed in the Google OAuth consent screen.
